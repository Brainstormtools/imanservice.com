# Training participants, attendance and evidence

This is the current guide. Training definitions, attendee registration, actual attendance, attendance approval, Client confirmation and learning hours are separate records. A Draft roster, recorded participant name or legacy documentation review does not prove attendance. Choices, names, times, proof and reasons are entered manually; the system does not assign people, reviewers or signing authority.

## Define the actual session

For project-linked training, open **Project training documentation** in the Configurator. Save the actual topic/objective as a Draft, then use **Revise training session definition** to explicitly choose Client or Internal and the actual active assigned facilitator. New choices start blank. Client sessions require their project. Type/facilitator revisions retain exact source versions, actual actor/server time/reason and immutable history. They do not grant project membership.

For standalone Internal sessions, open **Overtime & performance → Internal training**. HR/admin manage all these definitions; team leads manage their own, using existing facilitator/reporting scope. Enter the actual topic/objective, select an actual eligible staff facilitator and supply a reason. No project is required or created. The definition remains Draft even after separate attendance approval; a definition status is not the attendance review status.

The older **Revise training participants** project-team roster is a separate historical planning register. It is not the session attendee register, does not prove attendance and creates no access or credit. Its real account/source/reason history and private CSV remain available to authorized coordinators.

## Register actual attendees

Open **Session attendee register** on the actual project session definition or standalone Internal record. Select internal staff through real user accounts. External attendees use their actual name, phone and email; an account is not required. New full-list replacements start blank: select everyone who should remain, or explicitly clear the list with an empty selection and a reason. Existing eligibility, project/reporting scope, exact source version and previous revision are checked again by the server.

Registration alone creates no attendance, approval, client agreement or learning credit. Changes to the definition, facilitator, source or attendee register require a fresh current register/receipt. Earlier snapshots remain immutable; names and assignments are never copied into a new approved receipt automatically.

## Record actual attendance and proof

Open **Overtime & performance → Facilitated training**. Current selected facilitators see only their current sessions with attendee registers; project facilitators also need their current project assignment. Administrators can view and make reasoned corrections. Coordinator history is private.

Enter actual check-in and check-out for each attendee in your device timezone. Both times are required together; checkout must follow check-in and neither may be future. Leaving both blank means unrecorded, not absence. Save the complete time list with a fresh reason. Every correction retains the prior receipt and makes a new one; times are not silently copied to a different register.

On the exact saved attendance receipt, open **Private attendance proof**. Manually choose Photo or Signed attendance sheet, choose the actual file and enter a reason. Images may be PNG, JPEG or WebP; signed sheets may also be PDF. The technical upload limit is 10 MB. Basic file signatures are checked before upload. Registered proof cannot be overwritten or deleted through the application. An unchanged uncertain request can be retried without registering duplicate evidence. **Discard unregistered proof upload** cleans only an owned orphan, never registered evidence.

A new attendee/time/source receipt does not inherit old proof. Upload the appropriate actual evidence to the new receipt. The current-proof screen is for that exact current receipt; retained historical proof is inspected through the coordinator history view below.

## Submit and review attendance

Select actual proof files for the exact current receipt and supply the actual submission reason. Every registered attendee must have actual check-in/out pairs. Submission freezes the topic/objective, actual attendees/times and selected proof. Missing or changed submitted storage metadata blocks approval. A facilitator can reasonedly withdraw their own pending submission.

For Client sessions, use **Client training attendance submission** and **Overtime & performance → Client training review**. Approval requires the currently assigned PM's actual project assignment and PM permission. An unassigned administrator cannot approve as the PM. Administrators can inspect, return or withdraw Client review for correction. Approved withdrawal preserves the original approval event.

For Internal sessions, use **Internal training attendance submission** and **Overtime & performance → Internal training review**. Existing HR scope may review standalone and project-linked Internal sessions. A standalone creating team lead needs current reporting scope for the facilitator and registered internal attendees. A project team lead additionally needs actual assignment to that project. PM permission alone does not grant Internal review authority.

Choose each actual decision and enter its reason. Returned/withdrawn submissions and source/attendee/time corrections require fresh submission and review. Prior decisions stay immutable. Reviewers see submitted proof under their existing review scope; a project HR reviewer does not thereby receive coordinator source history or unselected files.

## Confirm a Client session

Administrators/current assigned PMs explicitly grant training signing authority to actual eligible client contacts under **Project training authorized signatories**. Grant, revoke or restore with a fresh reason. Training authority is independent of charter authority. Disabled or moved contacts are ineligible even if an old authority row exists. No signatory is chosen automatically.

Authorized clients open **Agreements → Client training confirmation**, inspect the exact current PM-approved receipt, type their actual full name, optionally enter a note and tick agreement. The receipt shows the frozen topic/objective, attendee names and actual times, source/attendance versions and PM approval identity/time. Agreement is initially unchecked. The server records the actual account/time and immutable evidence. No drawn signature, paid signing service or locked training PDF is required.

Stale source/attendance, withdrawn approval, revoked authority and missing/changed submitted proof block confirmation. Refresh after access/version errors. An unchanged failed request retains its identity; changed input receives a new one. An existing confirmation suppresses duplicate signing. A changed receipt needs fresh review/confirmation; old evidence stays historical. Export the saved confirmation receipt CSV. Active clients still in the project's company retain only their own historical evidence after authority revocation; disabled/transferred accounts lose access. Private contacts, staff IDs, proof paths/files and internal review notes are not exposed to clients.

## Inspect retained private receipt history

Authorized coordinators open the actual training record in **Project training documentation** or **Overtime & performance → Internal training**, then choose **Load training receipt history** under **Private training receipt history**. Loading is explicit; each record is scoped to that actual training source. The view includes retained registers, earlier source versions, attendance revisions and their original registered proof. Use **Export private training receipt history CSV** for the accessible frozen source/actor/time/reason/receipt/review/proof evidence.

**Current source/register** and **Current receipt** indicate freshness, not approval. Earlier registers/receipts are labelled historical. A corrected receipt has no proof until appropriate evidence is manually uploaded. Accessible review/confirmation history is included under existing row permissions; an empty list does not establish that a session was approved, returned or never reviewed.

Download historical registered proof using its private download button. The screen checks whether accessible storage metadata still matches the registered owner, size and MIME. This is not a cryptographic file-content integrity guarantee. Missing/changed metadata is flagged and the download is disabled; the immutable original proof metadata is retained. No new upload, delete, approval, correction or credit action is introduced by this history view.

Clients, ordinary attendees/facilitators and unassigned managers do not gain coordinator history access. A project PM without Internal review authority does not gain private Internal decisions; project HR review alone does not gain coordinator histories/unselected uploads. Role/account/project/reporting revocation is checked through the existing source, receipt, review, confirmation and storage row policies. Scope changes and failed reads/downloads clear old data/exports; late responses cannot restore an earlier private context.

## Understand source changes and legacy documentation status

Legacy project documentation can be Reviewed or Archived by an administrator with actual completion fields and a reason. This is separate from PM/lead/HR attendance review and Client confirmation. A documentation change increments the source version, so earlier registers, attendance, approvals and confirmations become historical rather than being silently promoted to the new source. Archived documentation is immutable. Authorized coordinators can still inspect retained receipts and original evidence.

Do not use legacy documentation review/archive as an attendance completion action. No separate standalone completion/archive policy or automatic certificate is configured by these features. The system does not infer that archiving a source preserves current credit or current signing eligibility. Definitions and corrections must follow the existing exact-source workflow; any additional completion/archive behaviour needs an explicit supported requirement.

## Inspect Internal learning credit

Current lead/HR-approved Internal receipts contribute actual elapsed time for their actual account-linked Internal staff attendees. External attendees, Client sessions, pending/returned reviews and stale/withdrawn/damaged receipts add no Internal credit. Original approved evidence remains historical after a correction. Correct actual attendance/proof and obtain fresh review instead of copying old approval.

Open **Overtime & performance → Learning credit**, choose the actual employee/month and inspect or export the credit basis. Staff see their own evidence; existing HR/reporting scope controls other accounts. Source/attendance/approval/selected-proof and existing approved Training activity identities support audit without exposing unrelated attendees or private evidence files. No hours, salary policy or KPI target is entered on this screen.

Actual elapsed time counts once: overlaps with already approved Training activities and other current Internal receipts are excluded, including across month boundaries. Time is not rounded and no break is assumed. Existing Pakistan start-month attribution is retained. Monthly scorecards include this learning-hours evidence; saved/locked summaries preserve their original basis. This calculation creates no new weighted training score, ordinary paid work activity, labour cost or overtime. Temporal overlap evidence is not an explicitly saved session-to-activity association.

## Link an existing Approved Training activity

In **Internal training attendance review**, open the exact submission and choose **Load activity associations**. The person making or removing a link must currently have review authority for both the Internal training receipt and the work activity. HR or team lead training permission alone does not grant activity review authority; project-manager activity permission alone does not grant Internal training review authority. No reviewer is assigned automatically.

Select an actual existing Approved Training activity for an actual internal attendee account, compare the displayed activity and attendance times, and enter a reason. The list starts blank. This is a manual evidence association, not an automatic assertion that the intervals fully match. The server checks the exact current approved Internal receipt, selected intact proof, actual staff account, activity version, approval and previous association revision again when saving.

The link creates no activity, labour cost, pay or extra learning hours. Existing independent activity approval and distinct-time learning calculations remain in force. Corrections, withdrawn training approval, damaged submitted proof, disabled staff or a voided activity make retained links inactive; history does not become a new approval. Use **Unlink activity association** with a reason to remove a latest link, including an inactive one. A replacement requires an explicit fresh selection/reason after unlinking. Prior actor/server time/reason and frozen receipt/activity/proof references remain available in private history and CSV only within current combined review authority. Refresh after a failure or scope change before another action.
