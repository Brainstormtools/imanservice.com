# Competence certificate records

Open **Overtime & performance → Competence certificates**, then **Load competence certificates**. Active internal employees see their own issued records; current HR/Admin can oversee all certificates and select eligible assessments for issuance. Assessors and reviewers do not gain certificate-issuer powers or unrelated certificate access. Training attendance, approvals and learning hours do not certify anyone.

HR/Admin selects an **Actual reviewed Pass assessment** from the blank field, then **Inspect selected issuance evidence**. Inspect the actual employee, exact criteria/checklist, completed recorded Pass and independent Team Lead/PM approval. Enter an actual **certificate action reason** and choose **Issue certificate from exact reviewed Pass**. This manual action records the actual signed-in HR/Admin issuer and server time; it does not choose or assign someone else as issuer. No criterion, qualification or person is supplied by development.

Fresh issuance requires the exact current assessment version, recorded receipt and latest independently Approved review, a current actual reviewer different from assessor, current designated assessor, active internal employee, current latest Active criteria and uncancelled assessment. Draft, incomplete, failed, unreviewed, returned, withdrawn, stale, forged or revoked/disabled authority cannot issue. A reviewed Fail stays Fail. Only one certificate may be issued from a particular assessment, including after revocation; another certificate needs a separately completed actual assessment and independent review.

The issued record freezes original actual employee/assessor/criteria, exact receipt/review, issuer role reference/reason/server time, administrator validity (default 12 months) and any explicitly configured warning lead time. Expiry uses UTC calendar months from server issuance. Blank warning days remain unconfigured; no threshold is invented. The issuance form cannot backdate or change settings, and later administrator criteria revisions do not rewrite issued evidence. The internal UUID reference identifies this actual record without inventing a business numbering scheme, accreditation or external certification claim.

Refresh evaluates **Current**, **Renewal due**, **Expired** or **Revoked** using server time. Renewal due starts at the exact explicitly configured warning date. Expired starts at the exact expiry time. Explicit revocation takes precedence and remains in history. This is an in-portal warning, with no email/SMS/WhatsApp or invented reminder schedule. Refresh to recheck time-sensitive status.

For an incorrect certificate, current HR/Admin opens its record, enters a real correction reason and chooses **Revoke certificate with retained evidence**. Original issuance and full revocation proof remain immutable, including account, server time and reason. No expiry edits, deletion, automatic replacement/restoration or copied renewal are offered. Later assessment or authority changes do not silently fabricate a certificate revocation; HR/Admin has this explicit correction action.

After uncertain failures, refresh the selected evidence. An unchanged action retains its request identity; if already committed, the original certificate/revocation acknowledges it without another write and clears saved inputs. Failed reads/writes remove private details/register/export until refreshed. Account/permission changes, switching away and back, unmount and delayed responses cannot restore old private data. **Export private certificate evidence CSV** contains the frozen issuance and any immutable revocation, not an inferred certificate layout or locked PDF.
