# Product reference coverage

Active Admin or Accounts (`finance.manage`) opens **Finance → Product coverage**, then chooses the actual PKR project. The project starts blank. The view reads the existing original accepted quotation reference and the four classified-cost families in one statement snapshot. It creates no financial or mapping record.

The identity table uses exact workstream and product IDs, with the cost's frozen target project checked against the chosen project. Product names never establish identity. Original and cost labels may differ after a rename; those labels remain visible separately. Repeated original quotation items may share one identity row and their original quoted amounts sum there, while each cost split is counted once. Original-only and cost-only identities remain visible. Missing current family evidence is not displayed as zero or proof of cost completeness.

Expense, Labour, Stock and Office amounts remain in separate columns and source-family subtotals. No combined cost, recognized revenue, contribution, margin or profit is calculated. Every original item—including an unattributed commercial adjustment—and every scoped cost segment—including signed unallocated project-source remainders—is retained in the detail tables and export. If original quotation evidence is unavailable, current cost evidence remains visible without inventing zero revenue.

Project Expense/Labour/Stock scope comes from each actual source's project. Office sources remain General office. Only their current eligible applied slices targeting the selected project enter scoped Office amounts. Each such original Office source appears once with its selected slices summed. Other-target slices, the General office remainder and the full original Office amount appear in a separate context-only table and are never added to the selected project's costs. Stale Office sources without eligible current target slices remain in the General office register; this view does not imply that no other Office cost exists.

**Export product reference coverage CSV** retains the loaded original/read/project/publication identities and limitations, stable-identity grid, all original items, scoped source IDs/application states, all cost segments and excluded Office context. Unknown original values remain Not recorded. An empty/unavailable original reference can still be exported as coverage evidence with explicit unknown values and any actual current costs. CSV escapes manual wording and does not duplicate private quote budget/unit-cost/vendor/margin snapshots or private uploaded files.

Refresh clears the project choice and previous report. Failed reads/exports, account/authority changes and late prior-project/account responses cannot restore earlier private evidence/exports. Clients, workers and PM-only accounts receive no new access. Source approval, original frozen mapping, applied classification, withdrawal, stale reconciliation and negative stock rules remain with the existing source reports.

This is a coverage comparison, not an operative financial posting or completeness certificate. No new assignment, reviewer, price/rounding/distribution policy, source ledger, cash/invoice/tax/WHT treatment, project financial total or BOQ adoption is introduced. Accepted additional quoted references remain separate. Broader DEV-042 product financials/operative treatment and all client-owned signed-in/live/browser/export/device acceptance remain pending.
