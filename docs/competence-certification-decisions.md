# Competence and certification: confirmed decisions

Confirmed by the client on 19 October 2026 for DEV-027. Administrator criteria/settings and assessor-designation setup are the first implementation increment; see [Competence configuration](competence-configuration-guide.md). Certification itself is not deployed. Existing training attendance, approval and learning credit do not certify competence.

Each competency has an actual practical checklist. Every step records Pass or Fail, with mandatory steps identified in the competency definition. A quiz is optional. Passing requires all mandatory steps to pass and, when a quiz is used, its actual score to meet or exceed the administrator-configured pass mark (default 70%). Nonmandatory checklist failures do not replace the stated mandatory-step rule. Actual checklists, questions, answers and employee assessment results remain manual business content.

The assessor is a Site Engineer or senior technician. The reviewer is a Team Lead or PM and must be a different account from the assessor. The issuer is HR or Admin. These role rules do not select actual people, designate a technician as senior, or assign reviewers. Implement explicit eligible-account selection and current-authority checks; never infer a senior designation from a name, attendance or unrelated access.

Certificate validity is administrator-configured, default 12 months. Show an expiry warning before renewal. No warning lead time or reminder-delivery policy was specified: provide an explicit administrator setting rather than inventing a threshold or external message. Certificate issuance adds the skill to the employee's skill profile for Configurator resource suggestions. Actual resource selection/assignment stays manual. Assessments, independent review, issuance, expiry/renewal and profile/suggestion effects require their own tested implementation and immutable evidence.

Precise implementation resume: inspect existing staff skills and Configurator suggestion permissions, then build a bounded, versioned competence-definition/assessment/review/issuance flow using these confirmed rules. Preserve actual accounts, practical outcomes, optional actual quiz score, configured criteria and validity, distinct assessor/reviewer and issuer evidence. Do not infer competency, certificate standards, salaries, working-hour rules or KPI targets from approved training or learning hours.
